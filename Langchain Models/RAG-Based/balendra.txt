**Balendra Shah**, popularly known as **“Balen”**, is a Nepali politician, structural engineer, and former rapper. As of **September 2026**, he is the **Prime Minister of Nepal** and a senior leader of the **Rastriya Swatantra Party (RSP)**. ([Gorkhapatra News][1])

### His background

* **Born:** April 27, 1990, in Kathmandu.
* **Education:** BE in Civil Engineering and M.Tech in Structural Engineering; he has also been pursuing doctoral research at Kathmandu University. ([Balen for Nepal][2])
* **Before politics:** He worked as a structural engineer and became known in Nepal's underground hip-hop scene as **Balen**. ([ABC News][3])
* **Kathmandu Mayor:** He won the 2022 Kathmandu mayoral election as an **independent candidate**, defeating candidates from established political parties. ([Gorkhapatra News][4])
* **RSP:** In January 2026, he resigned as mayor and joined the Rastriya Swatantra Party to contest the parliamentary election. ([Radio Nepal Online][5])
* **Prime Minister:** Following the 2026 general election, he became Prime Minister on **March 27, 2026**. ([Gorkhapatra News][1])

### Why did he become famous?

His rise was unusual because he **didn't come from the traditional political establishment**. He first became popular through music and engineering, then entered local politics and won Kathmandu's mayoral election independently.

As mayor, his public agenda included issues such as **waste management, urban discipline, traffic, illegal structures, and municipal transparency**. ([Balen for Nepal][2])
